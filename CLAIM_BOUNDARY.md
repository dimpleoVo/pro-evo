# Claim boundary

Supported public claim: under three prospectively frozen same-checkpoint pre-revision comparisons, treatment achieved target resolution and verified completion in 3/3 branches while generic recovery achieved 0/3; three treatment branches expose complete public mechanism chains.

Not supported: universal improvement, cross-model performance, statistical generalization, production proof, industry-wide superiority, or claims about all tasks or agents. Cross-model generalization is **not established**. This is a strong causal proof-of-concept under the frozen experimental condition, with a small sample.
