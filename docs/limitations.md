# Limitations

This release contains three frozen matched pairs in the Strong Mechanism Attribution and Corrective Optimization Validation and is not a cross-model, cross-Provider, or population-level study. It does not establish universal improvement, production readiness, statistical significance, or industry-wide state of the art. Public Evidence is intentionally reduced to preserve privacy and proprietary boundaries; it enables audit of the stated aggregates and method, not reconstruction of the full Runtime.
